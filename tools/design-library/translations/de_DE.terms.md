# German terms for design and setup words

The term list a translator follows, so one idea reads one way across every
design. The words themselves are in `de_DE.po` (ADR 0183). `CONTEXT.md` settles
the English; this settles the German.

## Voice

- **Informal "du", lower case in the middle of a sentence.** WordPress's `de_DE`
  is informal, and `de_DE_formal` reads this list too, because a site gets one
  list per language.
- Warm, short and direct, the way a shop writes to a customer. Not literal.
- Say a thing as short as the English says it. A button, an eyebrow or a badge
  must stay about as long as its English. Longer text can break a layout, and
  nothing checks it.
- **Keep what the English does:** ALL CAPS stays ALL CAPS, a line break stays
  where the sentence still breaks well, a missing full stop stays missing.
- Sentence case for headlines, with German noun capitals. No English title case.

## Consent sentences

A consent sentence is the visitor speaking, so it addresses nobody and says what the
visitor agrees to, faithfully, adding and dropping nothing:

- `Email me the guide.` → `Ich möchte den Leitfaden per E-Mail erhalten.`
- `Contact me about this request.` → `Ich möchte zu dieser Anfrage kontaktiert werden.`
- `Call me about this request.` → `Ich möchte zu dieser Anfrage angerufen werden.`
- `Use my details to respond to this request.` → `Meine Angaben dürfen genutzt werden, um auf diese Anfrage zu antworten.`

A button stays an instruction: `Send me the guide` → `Schick mir den Leitfaden`.

## Marks and numbers

- `%s`, `%b` and `%i` stay, once each, where the sentence needs them. `%s` is
  usually a link to the privacy policy at the end of a sentence.
- A number with a percent sign takes a no-break space: `10 %`. Never `100%ig`,
  which reads as a mark.
- Amounts and currency signs stay exactly as written, as do dates of a sale's
  days: `November 27–30` becomes `27.–30. November`.
- German quotes: `„…“`. Keep the typographic apostrophe `’` where one is needed.

## What never changes

- Sample brand and product names (`Fern & Form`, `Maison Arden`), people's names
  (`Alex Morgan`), and `Black Friday`, `Cyber Monday`, `Halloween`.
- `you@example.com` becomes `du@example.com`.
