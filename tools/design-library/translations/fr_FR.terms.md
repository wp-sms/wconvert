# French terms for design and setup words

The term list a translator follows, so one idea reads one way across every
design. The words themselves are in `fr_FR.po` (ADR 0183). `CONTEXT.md` settles
the English; this settles the French.

## Voice

- **"Vous", always.** It is what French shops write, and `fr_CA` and `fr_BE` read
  this list too, because a site gets one list per language.
- Warm, short and direct, the way a shop writes to a customer. Not literal.
- Say a thing as short as the English says it. A button, an eyebrow or a badge
  must stay about as long as its English. Longer text can break a layout, and
  nothing checks it.
- **Keep what the English does:** ALL CAPS stays ALL CAPS, a line break stays
  where the sentence still breaks well, a missing full stop stays missing.
- Sentence case. No English title case. Accents stay on capitals: `ÉTÉ`, `À`.

## Consent sentences

A consent sentence is the visitor speaking, so it says what the visitor agrees to
in the first person, faithfully, adding and dropping nothing:

- `Email me the guide.` → `Je souhaite recevoir le guide par e-mail.`
- `Contact me about this request.` → `Je souhaite être contacté(e) au sujet de cette demande.`
- `Call me about this request.` → `Je souhaite être rappelé(e) au sujet de cette demande.`
- `Use my details to respond to this request.` → `Mes coordonnées peuvent être utilisées pour répondre à cette demande.`

A button stays an instruction: `Send me the guide` → `Recevoir le guide`.

## Marks and numbers

- `%s`, `%b` and `%i` stay, once each, where the sentence needs them. `%s` is
  usually a link to the privacy policy at the end of a sentence.
- A number with a percent sign takes a no-break space: `10 %`. Never put `%`
  directly before the letters s, b or i, which reads as a mark.
- No-break space before `:`, `;`, `!`, `?` and inside `« »`.
- Amounts and currency signs stay exactly as written. Dates read the French way:
  `November 27–30` becomes `27–30 novembre`.
- French quotes: `« … »`. Use the typographic apostrophe `’`.

## What never changes

- Sample brand and product names (`Fern & Form`, `Maison Arden`), people's names
  (`Alex Morgan`), and `Black Friday`, `Cyber Monday`, `Halloween`.
- `you@example.com` becomes `vous@example.com`.
