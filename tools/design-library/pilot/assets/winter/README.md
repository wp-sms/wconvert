# Winter illustrations

These WebP exports reproduce the user-approved Christmas and New Year prototype artwork. The thirty geometric illustrations originate in `winter-prototype/art.mjs`; the cat, Santa and ribbon card were generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. Exact prompts are in `winter-prototype/assets/cards/provenance.json`. External reference artwork is not included.

Exports preserve the whole composition and transparency, use at most 1200 × 1000 pixels, and were encoded as WebP at quality 90. Text, dates, forms, codes and buttons are native elements outside the images. The source PNGs remain in the prototype. Image SHA-256 provenance and Free/premium access are recorded in `publishing/catalog.json`. An asset shared with a hosted Free design is public; exclusively Pro artwork is premium.
