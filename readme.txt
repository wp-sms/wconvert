== Description ==

**WConvert is a popup builder and optin form plugin for WordPress.** Collect email addresses, phone numbers and enquiries with popups and inline forms that look like they belong on your site.

Tell WConvert what you want, such as more newsletter subscribers, more enquiries or more downloads of your guide. It hands you a ready-made campaign with the words, fields and timing already filled in. Change anything you like, then publish. It's a head start, not a blank canvas.

No account to create. No limit on views or leads. Your leads stay in your own WordPress database.

= Why WConvert? =

* **Start from a goal, not a blank canvas.** Pick what you want to achieve and get a working campaign, words included, in a few clicks.
* **Forms are built in.** Email, phone and enquiry fields, consent checkboxes and multistep signups come with the plugin. You don't need a separate form plugin.
* **No caps.** Free has no limit on campaign views, signups or leads, and there is no monthly plan to outgrow.
* **Your leads stay on your site.** Every submission is saved in your WordPress database, with CSV export. WConvert never sends your leads to us.
* **Light on your pages.** With no campaign published, WConvert adds nothing to your pages. With one published, it loads a single plain-JavaScript file of about 17 KB, compressed, with no jQuery.
* **Polite to your visitors.** Built-in frequency limits stop a popup from showing again and again, and can stop all campaigns once someone signs up.
* **Privacy built in.** WConvert uses no visitor tracking IDs and stores no IP addresses. It records the exact consent wording each visitor saw.

= How WConvert compares =

**OptinMonster** is a hosted service. You build campaigns in its app, you need an OptinMonster account, and every plan comes with a monthly allowance of campaign views. WConvert runs entirely inside WordPress. It needs no account, and neither Free nor Pro has a view limit.

**Popup Maker** is a well-loved popup plugin that leaves forms to other plugins. For phone fields, enquiry forms or multistep signups, you add a separate form plugin. WConvert builds those in, along with a lead log and CSV export.

*Checked October 2026. Other products change, so see their own sites for current details. Full comparisons are at [wconvert.io/compare](https://wconvert.io/compare/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=compare).*

= Start from a goal =

Choose what you want to achieve, and WConvert suggests ready-made setups that do it:

* **Grow my email list.** Collect email addresses for your newsletter with an email popup or signup form.
* **Grow my SMS list.** Collect phone numbers for text messages, with a proper international phone field.
* **Collect enquiries.** Get quote requests, bookings and questions, along with contact details.
* **Deliver a lead magnet.** Email a guide, checklist or PDF download automatically when someone signs up.
* **Promote an offer or content.** Send visitors to a sale, a coupon, a page or a post.

Every setup comes with copy written for that goal. You fill in your own brand and details.

= Popups and inline forms =

* **Popups** that open on top of the page and match any theme.
* **Inline forms** inside your posts and pages. Add the WConvert campaign block, or paste a shortcode into any page builder, widget or theme file.
* **Multistep signups.** Ask for an email first, then a phone number or a question on the next step.
* **Mobile-friendly.** Every design works on phones as well as on desktop, and you can preview both before you publish.

= 150+ ready-made designs =

* **Over 150 designs** cover newsletters, coupons, lead magnets, enquiries, bookings and announcements. All the artwork is original and drawn in the page, so no image is loaded from another site.
* **More than 80 designs ship inside the plugin** and work offline from day one.
* **The rest come from the online [WConvert library](https://wconvert.io/templates/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=designs)**, once you allow it. Add a design with a click, and it then works offline like the rest. Some designs need WConvert Pro.
* **Click any part of a design to change it**: words, colours, fields and buttons, right beside a live preview.
* **Move designs between sites.** Export a design to a file and import it on another site.

= Show it to the right people, at the right time =

* **When it opens:** as the page loads, after a few seconds, after the visitor scrolls, or when they stop moving for a while.
* **Where it shows:** every page, or only the posts, pages, categories or addresses you pick, or everywhere except those.
* **Who sees it:** everyone, logged-in visitors, guests, or particular user roles such as customers or members.
* **Phones or desktop only**, and **opening hours**, so it shows only during the times you choose.
* **Start and end dates**, so a sale campaign switches itself off on time.
* **Don't annoy anyone:** limit how often each visitor sees a campaign, and set one rule for the whole site, for example "nothing more once they sign up".
* **Find out why a popup didn't show:** add `?wconvert-inspect=1` to any page address while logged in as an admin, and WConvert tells you which rule held it back.

= See what works =

* **One screen for what WConvert brought to your site**: leads captured, clicks and times shown.
* **Results by goal and by campaign**, with a chart of each day.
* **Compare with the previous period** to see if you're growing.
* **Monthly targets**, for example 100 new subscribers this month.

Everything is counted on your own site. There is no tracking service and no visitor ID.

= Your leads, in one place =

* **Every submission in a lead log**, with search, filters and CSV export.
* **Send email signups to MailPoet** if you use it. Mailchimp, Brevo and webhooks for Zapier and Make come with Pro.
* **Lead magnet delivery:** email a download link automatically, with no extra service.

= Privacy and GDPR tools =

* **The consent each visitor gave** is saved with their submission, using the exact words they saw on the form.
* **WordPress's own personal-data export and erasure** tools find and remove a person's submissions.
* **A retention period you choose**, after which old submissions are deleted.
* **Suggested text for your privacy policy**, added to WordPress's privacy policy guide.
* **No IP addresses stored, and no visitor tracking.** Nothing is loaded from other sites unless you turn it on.

These tools help you meet GDPR and similar laws. How you use them on your site is up to you.

= Spam protection =

Hidden-field checks and submission limits are built in and on by default. For extra protection, turn on Cloudflare Turnstile, Google reCAPTCHA or hCaptcha with your own keys.

= Source code =

The admin screens, the visitor-facing loader and the editor block are built with React, TypeScript and Vite. The compiled files in `public/` are generated from the readable sources in the `resources/` folder of the [plugin's GitHub repository](https://github.com/wp-sms/wconvert), which also holds the build tools:

* `public/loader/` and `public/inspector/` come from `resources/loader/src`
* `public/admin/` comes from `resources/admin/src`
* `public/blocks/` comes from `resources/blocks/inline-optin/src`
* `public/phone/` comes from `resources/phone/src`
* `public/protection/` comes from `resources/protection/src`

To rebuild them, run `npm ci && npm run build:free` in a clone of the repository, using Node.js 22.

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins → Add New**, search for **WConvert**, and click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.
2. Open **WConvert** in the admin menu and click **Create campaign**.
3. Pick a goal, choose a ready-made setup, change anything you like, and publish.

Guide: [Create your first campaign](https://wconvert.io/docs/create-your-first-campaign/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=install).

**WConvert Pro:** install it alongside the free plugin. Pro adds to WConvert and does not replace it.

**Multisite:** activate WConvert on each site on its own. Network activation is not supported and is refused with a message.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is WConvert really free? Is it a trial? =

Yes, it's free, and no, it isn't a trial. The free plugin is complete on its own and never expires. It has no limit on campaigns' views, signups or leads, and no account to create. WConvert Pro is an optional add-on for more formats, triggers and integrations.

= Do I need an account or another service? =

No. Everything works inside WordPress, with nothing else installed. MailPoet is used only if you already have it. The online design library is optional and asks before it loads anything; see External services.

= How is WConvert different from OptinMonster or Popup Maker? =

OptinMonster is a hosted service that needs an account and limits campaign views by plan. WConvert runs inside WordPress, with no account and no view limits. Popup Maker relies on a separate form plugin for phone, enquiry and multistep forms. WConvert builds them in, with a lead log. See [the full comparisons](https://wconvert.io/compare/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= How do I put a form inside a post? =

In the block editor, add the **WConvert campaign** block and pick your campaign by name. Not using the block editor? The block shows a shortcode you can paste anywhere, such as a page builder, a widget or a theme file:

`[wconvert_optin id="YOUR_CAMPAIGN_ID"]`

Popups don't need this. They show on the pages you choose by themselves.
Guide: [Add an inline campaign with a block or shortcode](https://wconvert.io/docs/add-inline-campaign/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= Does it work with my theme or page builder? =

Popups are added by WConvert itself, so they appear on top of any theme. For inline forms, page builders such as Elementor, Divi and Beaver Builder take the shortcode in their shortcode element. In the block editor and block themes, use the WConvert campaign block.

= Can I show a popup when someone is about to leave (exit intent)? =

Exit-intent and scroll-up triggers are part of WConvert Pro. In the free plugin you can open a campaign after a delay, after scrolling, or when the visitor stops moving for a while. That last option often reaches the same people.
Guide: [Exit-intent and scroll-up triggers](https://wconvert.io/docs/exit-and-scroll-up-triggers/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= How do I stop a popup from showing again and again? =

Each campaign has its own limits, for example once per visit, or never again after someone signs up. Under **Settings** you can also set a limit for the whole site, so visitors never see too many campaigns.
Guide: [Limit how often visitors see campaigns](https://wconvert.io/docs/limit-campaign-frequency/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= Can I show a popup only on some pages? =

Yes. Pick the posts, pages, categories or page addresses where it should show, or where it should not.
Guide: [Show a campaign on selected pages](https://wconvert.io/docs/show-on-selected-pages/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= Can I collect phone numbers for SMS marketing? =

Yes. Add a phone field to any form. It formats international numbers and checks them as visitors type. The numbers are kept in your lead log, and you can export them to CSV for your SMS service. With WConvert Pro, a webhook can send them to your SMS service as each person signs up.

= How do I send signups to Mailchimp, MailPoet or my CRM? =

MailPoet connects in the free plugin. Mailchimp, Brevo and Mailtrap connect with WConvert Pro, and so do webhooks, which reach Zapier, Make, n8n and most CRMs. Every signup is also saved in your lead log.
Guide: [Choose where submissions go](https://wconvert.io/docs/choose-where-submissions-go/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= How do I give away a PDF, guide or checklist? =

Pick the **Deliver a lead magnet** goal. WConvert emails a download link to each person who signs up, using your site's own email. No extra service is needed.

= Will it slow down my site? =

If no campaign is published, WConvert adds nothing to your pages. When one is, it loads a small plain-JavaScript file of about 17 KB, compressed, with no jQuery. Forms with a phone field add a small helper for phone numbers. Nothing is loaded from other sites unless you turn on a bot-verification service or remote fonts.

= Is it GDPR friendly? Does it store IP addresses or track visitors? =

WConvert gives you the tools: it saves the consent wording each visitor saw, works with WordPress's personal-data export and erasure, deletes old submissions after a period you choose, and suggests privacy policy text.

It uses no visitor ID and does no tracking across pages; results are daily totals for each campaign. To slow down spam, a scrambled, one-way code made from the visitor's network address is kept for a few minutes. The address itself is never saved.
Guide: [What WConvert stores, sends and counts](https://wconvert.io/docs/what-wconvert-stores/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= Does it work with WooCommerce? =

Yes. The free plugin can show campaigns on shop, product and category pages, for example a first-order coupon for new customers. WConvert Pro adds cart recovery campaigns, product recommendations and quizzes that suggest products.

= My popup isn't showing. What do I check? =

Open the page while logged in as an admin and add `?wconvert-inspect=1` to its address. WConvert lists every rule and shows which one held the campaign back. A caching plugin may also need its cache cleared after you publish.
Guide: [Popup not showing](https://wconvert.io/docs/popup-not-showing/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= Where do my leads go? =

Into your own WordPress database, and also to MailPoet or a Pro integration if you set one up. WConvert never sends your leads to us or to anyone else.

= How do I delete someone's data? =

Use WordPress's own tools under **Tools → Erase Personal Data**. They delete that person's submissions. To remove a phone number only, search for it in **WConvert → Leads** and delete the matching submissions there. Copies in other services, in downloaded files and in backups need removing there too.

Under **WConvert → Settings → Data & privacy** you can see where visitor data goes and set how long submissions are kept.
Guide: [Erase someone's personal data](https://wconvert.io/docs/erase-personal-data/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

= What happens if I delete the plugin? =

Deactivating changes nothing, and everything is still there when you turn it back on. Deleting the plugin removes all of its data, including your leads, and this cannot be undone. Export your leads to CSV from **WConvert → Leads** first if you want to keep them.
Guide: [Deactivate or uninstall WConvert](https://wconvert.io/docs/deactivate-or-uninstall/?utm_source=wordpress.org&utm_medium=plugin-directory&utm_campaign=wconvert-free&utm_content=faq).

== External services ==

WConvert works without an account or any external service. Nothing below is contacted unless an administrator turns it on.

**Online design library.** The designs included in the plugin work offline and
need nothing else. The online WConvert library at https://templates.wconvert.io
is on by default but **asks first**: before anything is fetched, the design
picker shows a one-time question, and the answer is kept in Settings → Data &
privacy, where it can be changed at any time. Nothing is fetched on activation,
from cron, on the dashboard or on your site's pages. While the picker is open,
WConvert checks for new designs at most twice a day.

What is fetched: JSON design and setup data and PNG, JPEG or WebP images. Every
file is checked against a SHA-256 checksum, and nothing executable or any font
is downloaded. A design you add is stored under `wp-content/uploads/` and keeps
working offline. Your browser loads design thumbnails from the same address with
no referrer, so the service sees your browser's IP address.

What is sent: requests from the free plugin carry no licence key, site address,
campaign, lead or visitor data; the server's IP address, the requested address
and a fixed user agent are all the service sees. When a design is previewed or added, the
service counts that download for that design, with no site or visitor
identifier. **WConvert Pro** also sends its licence key and your site's domain to
the same service when you add a Pro design, and the service asks the WConvert
licence server (Nexus, at https://my.wconvert.io) to issue a five-minute pass for
those files.

The service runs on Cloudflare, which processes these requests for us. Its
request logs are kept for at most 7 days and download counts for 3 months.

To turn the library off for the whole site, add
`define( 'WCONVERT_TEMPLATE_CATALOG', false );` to `wp-config.php`, or return
`false` from the `wconvert_template_catalog` filter. An operator can also point
it at another catalogue address; the trusted recipient of a Pro licence key does
not change with it.

Service terms: https://wconvert.io/terms/
Privacy policy: https://wconvert.io/privacy/

**Bot verification.** Hidden-field checks and submission limits are built in.
In Settings → Spam protection, administrators may enable one verification
service using their own account and keys: Cloudflare Turnstile Managed, Google
reCAPTCHA v2 checkbox, or hCaptcha. No service is selected by default. Provider
charges and quotas are separate from WConvert.

The selected service loads when a form needs verification or an administrator
runs Test saved setup. Its script receives browser and network information,
including the visitor's IP address. WConvert sends the verification token and
required credentials from the server to that service; it does not forward the
form's email, phone or other captured answers. The server also checks the
provider response before accepting a protected form. Visitors can retry failed
verification without losing their entered fields. Administrators should review
the selected service's terms and their site's privacy notice before enabling it.

* Cloudflare Turnstile: https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/
  Terms: https://www.cloudflare.com/website-terms/
  Privacy: https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/
* Google reCAPTCHA: https://www.google.com/recaptcha/about/
  Terms: https://policies.google.com/terms
  Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* hCaptcha: https://www.hcaptcha.com/
  Terms: https://www.hcaptcha.com/terms
  Privacy: https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy

**Optional Google Fonts.** Remote Google Fonts are disabled by default. In Settings → Data & privacy,
the site owner can enable them after acknowledging the browser connection.
When an eligible campaign is shown, WConvert requests only its declared, used
fonts that are not provided by the site's font list. The active editor preview
also loads these fonts when enabled. Selecting or importing a design does not
enable this setting. Gallery cards do not initiate font requests.

Stylesheets are requested from fonts.googleapis.com and font files from
fonts.gstatic.com. Google receives the browser's IP address and HTTP request
information. WConvert sends no form answers to this service. Installing fonts
in the WordPress Font Library is the locally hosted alternative. Disabling
remote delivery prevents new requests on freshly served pages; clear host/CDN
page caches after changing the setting. Existing browser caches may retain fonts.

Service: https://fonts.google.com/
Terms: https://policies.google.com/terms
Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Queued resource emails are limited to one successful send per recipient and
resource within ten minutes. Separate submissions remain separate leads.
