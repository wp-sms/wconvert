Show something useful for this basket

WConvert Pro with WooCommerce can show a campaign based on products, categories, item quantity or merchandise amount in the current cart.

Example: coffee machine accessories

  1. Choose Promote an offer → Recommend a compatible accessory.
  2. Select the product block and choose your compatible filters, cleaning brush and storage jar. Put your preferred products first.
  3. Under Audience, add Cart products, choose the coffee machine, and use Any. You can instead select a product category, including its subcategories if needed.
  4. Place the inline campaign near the basket using its WordPress block or shortcode.
  5. Open Display rules → Test a sample visit. Add the machine to the sample basket, then add an accessory. That accessory should disappear from the preview. Check the live shop before publishing.

Up to three available products appear from your ordered list of six. Existing basket items are hidden by default. You choose compatibility; WConvert does not infer it.

Use your WooCommerce cross-sells

In the product block, change Recommendation source to Use WooCommerce cross-sells. For example, link filters and a cleaning brush to your coffee machine in WooCommerce. WConvert then suggests those products when the machine is in the basket. It skips unavailable products and, by default, products already in the basket. If no linked products are eligible, the campaign stays hidden.

Test without changing a real cart

The sample basket uses your unsaved draft. Choose products or variations, set quantities and enter a pretend merchandise amount. Amounts are not calculated from product prices. Try an unavailable basket or consent not given to see why a campaign stays hidden. Categories, linked cross-sells and availability come from your store; other page and visitor conditions are explicit assumptions. No visit, click or purchase is recorded.

Other useful filters

Show bulk-order guidance when quantity is at least five. Show a relevant guide when a selected category is present. Use an amount range for merchandise after discounts, excluding tax and shipping. The saved amount uses your store currency and precision; review it if these change.

None means none of the selected products or categories is present. A known empty cart can satisfy that filter. Product recommendation campaigns additionally require a nonempty basket and at least one available suggestion.

What your numbers mean

The campaign counts a product-link click. It does not report a purchase, extra revenue or a recovered order. Variable products open their product page to choose options.

If the campaign does not appear

Check Pro and WooCommerce, selected products, Audience rules, placement and the live eligibility inspector. Missing, expired or unavailable cart data cannot match either positive or negative filters. Imported selected-product designs need products selected again on this store. Cross-sell designs use the receiving store’s configured relationships.

Core classic WooCommerce and Cart Blocks update automatically. Third-party carts without the normal events may wait for the next refresh. No cart contents are stored by WConvert.
