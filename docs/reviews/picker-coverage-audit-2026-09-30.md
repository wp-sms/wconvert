4. Review the four cart recovery setups on a real WooCommerce site before expanding them. Hide unsupported creation goals rather than treating WooCommerce as a paid-plan benefit.
5. Fullscreen has five examples. Add only for tasks that need the space, such as a useful preference guide, rather than making a fullscreen clone quota.
6. Review the 24 untagged setups and tag only evidenced use cases. Keep the three broad business groups. Proposed industries (fashion, beauty, home/garden, home services, education/coaching, fitness/wellness) need reviewed coverage before becoming customer filters.
7. Author real seasonal offer/terms/end-state copy and New Year examples before expanding the calendar. The current Black Friday collection is adaptable preparation guidance.

These are editorial recommendations based on supply coverage, not proof of customer demand or conversion improvements. A new setup needs a distinct visitor task; a new design needs a distinct composition.
