# Christmas & New Year: 40 native templates

The approved prototype is implemented as 40 native designs and matching Playbooks: 30 Pro, 10 hosted Free, 55 screens including 15 acknowledgements. The ten Free designs are not bundled in either plugin ZIP. The Christmas & New Year collection lists the whole batch; installation and plan access use the existing catalogue controls.

Text, dates, year numbers, labels, fields, consent, links, coupon controls and clocks use the shipping vocabulary. Illustrations are replaceable WebP assets. Native decorations supply optional float/blink sparkles in 13 designs and respect the renderer's reduced-motion behavior. No custom frontend script, CSS or animation engine is introduced.

## Specific visual decisions

The final shipping-renderer screens were inspected at 320 and 1440 px, including all acknowledgements and native dismiss controls. The checklist covers intent, reward, truthful urgency and acknowledgement, hierarchy, practical format, theme variation and distinct composition. Longer copy and both text directions were measured across eight widths. The native adaptations below preserve the approved design intent while keeping the content editable.

## Native verification

- Standard and longer-copy audits: 3,520 cases across designs and filled setups, 320/390/520/640/768/960/1280/1440 px, LTR and RTL, with no overflow, close collisions, small controls or contrast findings. See `layout-audit.json`.
- Midnight and Minimal theme checks: 220 screen/width cases including RTL; no contrast or field-boundary failures. See `themes.json`.
- Real WordPress journey checks exercise all 40 setups at 320 and 1280 px. Signup paths reject empty/invalid email and missing consent, retain details through a simulated failure, retry successfully, save a local Lead and show the acknowledgement. Configured links and native coupon copy controls are exercised. See `results.json`.
- Sixteen countdowns at two viewport widths tick against the saved campaign end, hide zero-day boxes and keep their deadline across navigation. Offer-only screens close on expiry; the single timed signup preserves an open form and accepts its submission, but does not reopen after expiry. See `countdowns.json`. Fake-clock tests use the existing WordPress navigation-performance shim; the journey run uses the real browser clock.
- Explicit countdown dates in the examples are editable guidance, not independent timers. Merchants must set the campaign schedule and match the displayed wording. Coupon values and destinations must be configured; the templates do not create shop coupons or guarantee email delivery.

## Asset and packaging policy

`tools/design-library/pilot/assets/winter/README.md` records artwork provenance. Thirty geometric exports originate in the approved prototype; the ribbon card, ginger cat and Santa artwork were generated with ImageGen. External reference images are not shipped. Artwork is optimized WebP, no larger than 1200 × 1000, approximately 574 KB across 33 source exports. Shared Free artwork is public; exclusively Pro artwork stays private in the catalogue.

Fonts are declared through the existing font support and retain local fallbacks; these designs do not enable remote-font permission. Native motion can be hidden or edited in the normal decoration controls. The coupon acknowledgement uses a secondary Continue browsing action.

Final local suites: 3,728 PHP tests, 5,136 JavaScript tests, 90 design-studio tests, ESLint, TypeScript, template registration, source contract and current catalogue/collection revisions.
